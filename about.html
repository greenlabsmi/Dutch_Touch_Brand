About Us

Dutch Touch Genetics is a Michigan-based craft cannabis brand focused on award-winning flower, sustainable cultivation, and uncompromising quality. With multiple High Times Cannabis Cup wins, we believe world-class cannabis only happens with discipline, precision, and love for the plant.

Every cultivar is guided by terpene-driven selection, clean growing practices, and the Dutch heritage that inspired our name. Welcome to Dutch Touch Genetics.
